About

We bring three kinds of work under one roof: communication, interior design and online workshops and conferences, so a client can present a brand, shape a space and teach an audience with one team.

Service 1 · Brand and communication

Content that gets a brand noticed.

We plan, shoot and publish the material a business needs to speak to its audience, and we keep its social accounts active.

  • Content creationWritten and visual content for websites, social platforms and campaigns.
  • Video productionShort and long videos, from script and filming to editing.
  • Social media managementPlanning, publishing and following up your accounts.
Service 2 · Interior design and decoration

Interiors planned like a project, finished like a home.

We design and decorate interior spaces for homes and businesses, from the layout on the plan to the final furnishing.

  • Space planningLayouts that suit how the space will be used.
  • Interior designMaterials, colours, lighting and furniture chosen as one scheme.
  • DecorationThe finishing layer that makes a space ready to live or work in.
Service 3 · Online workshops and conferences

Teach, share and gather, wherever people are.

We organise workshops and conferences that take place online. Participants receive access to each session over the internet, from any country.

  • Online workshopsPractical sessions around a topic, led live.
  • Online conferencesTalks and panels delivered to a remote audience.
  • Session accessParticipants get access to the sessions they sign up for.
How we work

The same four steps on every project.

  1. Brief

    We listen to what you want, who it is for and what the deadline is.

  2. Concept

    We propose a direction and agree it with you before production starts.

  3. Production

    We create the content, the design or the session programme.

  4. Delivery

    We hand over the finished work and stay available for adjustments.
